## Thesis

When AI systems are embedded into institutional workflows, the organization that adopts and governs the workflow can be an appropriate responsibility subject.

LLM deploymentをmany-agents、many-levels、many-interactionsの問題として扱う[[constantinescuResponsibilityGapsLLMs2025]]は、この主張の直接的先行研究である。

本Argumentの追加点は、その分析をdesign、governance、oversight、risk allocationを担うorganizationの責任主体性へ接続する点にある。

## Argument

1. AI harms often emerge from organizational processes rather than from one isolated individual.
2. No single engineer, user, or operator may fully control the resulting behavior.
3. The organization can still be responsible for design choices, governance, oversight, training, monitoring, and risk allocation.
4. Therefore, organizational responsibility may be more appropriate than searching only for an individual culprit.

## Relevance

This argument is practically important for law and governance. It also intersects with philosophical questions about collective agency.
