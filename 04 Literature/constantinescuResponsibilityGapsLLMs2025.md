# Constantinescu and Kaptein 2025 - Responsibility Gaps, LLMs and Organisations

## Core Idea

Constantinescu and Kaptein propose a many-agents, many-levels, many-interactions approach for responsibility gaps in LLM deployment.

## Relevance to My Theory

This is directly relevant to LLM agents and organizational deployment. It supports analyzing responsibility as a multi-level interaction problem rather than a simple chain from user to model to harm.
