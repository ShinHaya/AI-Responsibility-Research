# Königs 2022 - Artificial Intelligence and Responsibility Gaps

## Core Idea

Königs asks what exactly the responsibility gap problem is supposed to be. The paper is useful as a critical check on strong claims that AI autonomy straightforwardly creates a complete responsibility gap.

---

## Relevance to My Theory

This paper helps sharpen [[Responsibility Gap]] by forcing the analysis to specify the missing responsibility condition:

- control?
- knowledge?
- foreseeability?
- culpability?
- accountability?
- sourcehood?
- institutional answerability?

For my theory, the most interesting missing condition is not merely control but responsible sourcehood.
