## Relevance to My Theory

<!-- section-provenance:project -->
**出自**：本研究による分析（比較、評価、AI責任論への応用または理論的拡張）。

This paper is relevant to the distinction between sophisticated agency-like behavior and genuine autonomy.
It is used in the conversation as a source for the claim that current AI may lack self-determination, autonomous goal formation, and critical self-reflection.

The paper anchors the [[LLM Sourcehood Problem]].

An LLM agent may plan and act competently while remaining heteronomous at the level of ends and values.

    Autonomous execution
        ≠
    Autonomous self-determination

This distinction is central to whether AI can be the responsible source of its actions.
