# Formosa, Hipólito, and Montefiore 2025 - AI, Agency, Autonomy, and Moral Patiency

## Core Idea

This paper is relevant to the distinction between sophisticated agency-like behavior and genuine autonomy. It is used in the conversation as a source for the claim that current AI may lack self-determination, autonomous goal formation, and critical self-reflection.

---

## Relevance to My Theory

The paper anchors the [[LLM Sourcehood Problem]].

An LLM agent may plan and act competently while remaining heteronomous at the level of ends and values.

    Autonomous execution
        ≠
    Autonomous self-determination

This distinction is central to whether AI can be the responsible source of its actions.
