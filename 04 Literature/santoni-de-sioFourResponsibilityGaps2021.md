# Santoni de Sio and Mecacci 2021 - Four Responsibility Gaps

## Core Idea

The responsibility gap is not one problem but a family of gaps, including culpability, moral accountability, public accountability, and active responsibility.

## Relevance to My Theory

This helps prevent treating AI responsibility as a single binary question. It supports separating [[Causal Responsibility]], [[Moral Responsibility]], [[Legal Responsibility]], and broader accountability practices.
