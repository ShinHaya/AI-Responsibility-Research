# Coeckelbergh 2020 - Responsibility Attribution and Explainability

## Core Idea

Coeckelbergh discusses AI responsibility attribution under the assumption that only humans are responsible agents, while emphasizing problems such as many hands, many things, and temporal complications of control.

## Relevance to My Theory

This supports a relational and distributed analysis of responsibility while remaining cautious about direct AI moral responsibility.
