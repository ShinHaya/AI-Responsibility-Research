# Floridi and Sanders 2004 - On the Morality of Artificial Agents

## Core Idea

Floridi and Sanders argue that artificial agents can be assessed as moral agents at an appropriate level of abstraction, without requiring human-like free will, emotions, or mental states.

## Relevance to My Theory

This is a useful contrast case. My current view treats artificial moral agency as insufficient for full moral or legal responsibility unless further conditions such as [[Sourcehood]], [[Reflective Self-Formation]], and [[Genetic and Diachronic Agency]] are met.
