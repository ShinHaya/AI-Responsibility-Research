# Lin et al. 2026 - Social-Oriented Motivation in Agents

## Core Idea

This work is relevant as an agent architecture that models desire values, belief updates, reflective reasoning, and value updates in social multi-agent interaction.

---

## Relevance to My Theory

Architectures of this kind approach some ingredients of artificial volitional structure:

    desire values
        ↓
    belief update
        ↓
    reflective reasoning
        ↓
    value update

However, the philosophical question remains whether such updates amount to self-owned, open-ended, diachronic volitional revision.
