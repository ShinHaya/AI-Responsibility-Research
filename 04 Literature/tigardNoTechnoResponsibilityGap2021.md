# Tigard 2021 - There Is No Techno-Responsibility Gap

## Core Idea

Tigard argues against the assumption that technology creates an unbridgeable responsibility gap, emphasizing the flexibility of responsibility practices.

## Relevance to My Theory

This is an important counterweight to Matthias. It supports the possibility that responsibility practices can be revised without immediately making AI itself a full responsible agent.
