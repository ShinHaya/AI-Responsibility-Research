## Core Idea

<!-- section-provenance:source -->
**出自**：文献由来（当該文献の内容を要約する欄。翻訳・要約の表現はノート作成者による）。

Matthias formulates the responsibility gap for learning automata: systems that learn and adapt can produce actions whose concrete form was not sufficiently predictable or controllable by designers or users.
