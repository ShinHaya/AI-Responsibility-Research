## Core Idea

<!-- section-provenance:source -->
**出自**：文献由来（@martelaArtificialIntelligenceFree2025の内容を要約する欄。翻訳・要約の表現はノート作成者による）。

Martela argues that generative agents using large language models can possess functional free will. The central move is to interpret free will at the level of agency rather than at the level of physics, hardware, or token sampling alone.

On this view, an LLM agent may count as having functional alternative possibilities when it recognizes multiple courses of action and selects among them in light of goals, plans, and environmental conditions.

---

## Key Claims

<!-- section-provenance:source -->
**出自**：文献由来（@martelaArtificialIntelligenceFree2025の内容を要約する欄。翻訳・要約の表現はノート作成者による）。

- The relevant level for free will may be the agency level, not the microphysical or computational level.
- Generative agents can operate with goals, plans, situational understanding, and action alternatives.
- Examples such as Minecraft agents suggest that LLM agents can choose among action paths such as exploration, construction, resource gathering, or avoidance.
- Functional free will does not require metaphysical contra-causal freedom.

## Relevance to My Theory

<!-- section-provenance:project -->
**出自**：本研究による分析（比較、評価、AI責任論への応用または理論的拡張）。

Martela is the strongest direct literature anchor for [[Agency-Level Alternative Possibilities]].

However, my project should treat Martela as necessary but insufficient. Martela can help establish the `Free` side of the model:

    Free = Agency-Level Alternative Possibilities

But moral responsibility also requires the `Will` side:

    Will = Frankfurtian Second-Order Volition + Sourcehood

Thus Martela becomes a target for constructive criticism:

> LLM agents may have functional free will in the sense of agency-level AP, but that does not yet establish artificial moral responsibility.

## My Evaluation

<!-- section-provenance:project -->
**出自**：本研究による分析（比較、評価、AI責任論への応用または理論的拡張）。

### Agree

Martela is right to resist reducing the AI free will question to token sampling or deterministic computation. Agency-level description matters.

### Disagree / Limitation

Functional alternative possibilities do not yet show that an AI is the source of its will. The account needs to explain ownership, self-attribution, second-order endorsement, and diachronic self-formation.
