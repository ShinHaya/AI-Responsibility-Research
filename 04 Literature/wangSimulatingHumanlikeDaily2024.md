# Wang et al. 2024 - Desire-driven Autonomy

## Core Idea

This work is relevant as an AI-agent architecture that attempts to generate tasks from internal desire-like states rather than only from external task prompts.

---

## Relevance to My Theory

Desire-driven agents are a possible computational bridge toward [[Frankfurtian Second-Order Volition in AI]], but they do not by themselves establish Frankfurtian volition.

A desire-like state is still first-order unless the agent can evaluate which desire should move it and can revise its motivational structure diachronically.
