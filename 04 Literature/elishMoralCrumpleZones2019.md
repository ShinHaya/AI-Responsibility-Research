## Core Idea

<!-- section-provenance:source -->
**出自**：文献由来（当該文献の内容を要約する欄。翻訳・要約の表現はノート作成者による）。

Elish introduces the moral crumple zone: the nearest human operator may absorb blame for failures of a complex automated system despite having limited control.

## Relevance to My Theory

<!-- section-provenance:project -->
**出自**：本研究による分析（比較、評価、AI責任論への応用または理論的拡張）。

This is a warning against solving AI responsibility gaps by assigning blame to whichever human remains nominally in the loop.
