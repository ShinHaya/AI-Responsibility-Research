## Abstract

Broadly neo-Aristotelian theories of the general ontology of objects and of causal powers and causation have proliferated in the past quarter century. This chapter begins by canvassing such theories and defending a particular position on each of these disputed topics. It then proposes a way to think about the exercise of (indeterministic) free will within the preferred framework.
