# Generating Meaning

## Bibliographic Information

- Authors: Giovanni Pezzulo, Thomas Parr, Paul Cisek, Andy Clark, Karl Friston
- Year: 2024
- Journal: Trends in Cognitive Sciences 28(2), 97-112
- DOI: 10.1016/j.tics.2023.10.002
- Zotero item key: `I6I7JF6S`

## Abstract

The paper contrasts generative AI's passive learned models with embodied organisms whose models must predict and control the sensory consequences of purposive action. By intervening in the world, organisms continuously test and revise their models; this grounding is argued to be important for genuine understanding.

## Core Thesis

Textやimage distributionを予測するだけのgenerative modelと、自らのactionが生むsensorimotor consequencesを予測・制御するactive modelは区別される。Meaningは、身体化されたagentが生存上重要なworld interactionを行い、その結果でmodelを試す循環に根ざす。

## Key Claims

- Living generative models are anchored to body and world.
- Active agents intervene rather than only predict observations.
- Action consequences provide endogenous tests of world models.
- Passive generative AI lacks this purposive sensorimotor anchoring.
- Embodied active inference may narrow the gap between statistical competence and understanding.

## My Evaluation

[[Agentive Action Initiation]]をoutput productionからworld interventionへ強化する。行為はmodelの結果であるだけでなく、将来の観測・学習・自己形成を生み出す。

ただし生命維持的self-evidencingやembodimentをmoral responsibilityへ直接結びつけることはできない。身体化されたgoal pursuitも、固定priorの遂行、道徳的盲目、他者軽視と両立する。意味生成から責任へはreflective and intersubjective bridgeが必要である。

## Questions Raised

- Robotic embodimentだけでpassive AIからactive agentへ移行するか。
- 人工agentのprior preferenceは生命的normativityのanalogueになりうるか。
- World interventionが責任的sourcehoodを強める条件は何か。
