## Bibliographic Information

---

## Core Idea

This paper argues that sampling from a probability distribution is not the same as choosing in the intentional, agency-involving, responsibility-relevant sense.

---

## Relevance to My Theory

This paper provides the strongest direct challenge to a thin reading of LLM alternative possibilities.

It supports the distinction:

    Output variability
        ≠
    Practical alternative possibilities
        ≠
    Responsible choice

The paper is useful against any argument that infers responsibility from stochastic generation or behavioral variability alone.

---

## My Evaluation

This paper should be read as a corrective to [[martelaArtificialIntelligenceFree2025]]. Martela is right to move to the agency level, but Keshet is right that multiple possible outputs do not automatically constitute choice.

The synthesis is:

    Sampling is not choosing,
    but agency-level planning may be more than sampling.

The burden is to identify the architecture that turns generated alternatives into reasons-owned alternatives.
