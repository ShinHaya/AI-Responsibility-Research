## Core Idea

A moral crumple zone is a situation in which a nearby human operator absorbs blame for failures produced by a complex automated or autonomous system, despite having limited control or understanding of the system's behavior.

The conversation introduces this through [[elishMoralCrumpleZones2019]]. The concept is especially important for automated driving and human-robot interaction, but it generalizes to LLM agents and tool-using AI systems.

## Relevance to AI Responsibility

The concept warns against an overly simple human-responsibility view. If responsibility is assigned only to the last human in the loop, the broader system of designers, deployers, institutions, models, tools, and governance structures may escape scrutiny.
